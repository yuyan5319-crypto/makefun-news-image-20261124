# The World in One Day — 24 November 2026

One image summing up the world news of Tuesday 24 November 2026, as a
newspaper front page with a masthead and six stories from around the
world:

1. **Technology — Nvidia:** the board reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO
   after Donald Trump intervenes; shares jump about a third in a day.
2. **War — Ukraine:** strikes on the Kyiv region kill at least 14.
3. **Politics — Washington:** Representative Jimmy Gomez resigns after
   a House Ethics panel finds evidence of sexual misconduct.
4. **AI — Grok:** US states sue Elon Musk's companies over sexualised
   images made with the Grok chatbot.
5. **Climate — Antalya, Türkiye:** COP31 talks stay deadlocked over
   fossil fuels as the summit runs past its deadline.
6. **Disaster — Indonesia:** more bodies recovered from the Banten
   candy factory fire.

The footer strip adds four more stories from the same day: Thanksgiving
travel chaos at US airports, Rishabh Pant's 128 in Wellington, the new
India–Nepal rail link, and Lisbon's cost-of-living march.

The image is drawn by hand with Pillow (flat pictograms and typeset
headlines) — no news photographs. `main.py` draws `entry.png` from
scratch and writes it to `/output/entry.png`; run it locally with
`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py`. Two runs write identical
bytes.
